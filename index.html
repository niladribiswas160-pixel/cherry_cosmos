The last page of this chapter

Dear Swastika,

If someone had told me in Class 1 that a girl I once saw at school would become such an important part of my life, I probably wouldn't have believed them.

I never imagined that I would search for someone called Buri, lose that little memory somewhere in time, and then find you again through a simple Instagram notification.

Our story was never a perfectly straight railway line. There were unexpected turns, difficult tracks, delays and moments we didn't understand.

But look how far this journey has brought us. I hope we keep collecting memories, discovering new places and making ordinary days feel special.

This website has eight stations and eight little photographs. But it could never contain every memory we have made.

So, this is not really a goodbye. It is just a pause before the next chapter.

With love,
Still writing our story. ♥
